# 🏙️ A2 Vibe: All-In-One Ann Arbor City Guide App

**A2 Vibe** is a high-performance, immersive mobile web application designed to act as the ultimate digital guide for Ann Arbor, Michigan. Featuring a modern, high-contrast dark/light mode architecture, real-time hydration and utility tools, curated local dining, live city happening trackers, and an integrated city journal, it delivers a seamless local experience.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend Framework:** React (Utilizing React Hooks, `useMemo`, and automated state orchestration)
* **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS, Lucide React Icons, and custom typography (`Outfit` & `Plus Jakarta Sans`)
* **State & Persistence:** LocalStorage synchronization for user favorites, hydration tracking, and instant UI preference updates.
* **Deployment Pipeline:** Automated continuous deployment via Vercel and GitHub.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Interactive City Exploration
* **The Scene & Insider Picks:** Curated local highlights, featured slideshows, and quick-launch categories for dining, events, and transit (TheRide).
* **City Happenings & Events:** Filterable local happenings with dynamic category sorting and expandable list items.
* **A2 Eats & Gear:** Discover local gourmet dining reviews and essential city gear items with direct purchasing links.

### 2. Urban Tools & Utilities
* **City Hot Spots:** Instant overview of Ann Arbor landmarks (The Michigan Stadium/The Big House, Nichols Arboretum, Law Quad, etc.).
* **Hydration Tracker:** Simple daily counter to track water and beverage intake on the go.
* **Tip Calculator:** Quick bill breakdown and customizable tip percentage tool.
* **City Forecast:** Interactive monthly weather and temperature index for planning visits across all seasons.

### 3. City Journal & Personal Hub ("My Vibe")
* **Categorized Reading:** Dynamic articles spanning city life, local secrets, arts & culture, and community reports.
* **Favorites Management:** Centralized hub to review saved gear, dining spots, and city attractions.
* **Theme Switching:** Instant toggle between a clean Light Mode and a high-contrast dark mode (`#050b14` / `#0a121e`).
