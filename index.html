A2 Vibe – Your Ann Arbor Insider

A2 Vibe is a free, mobile-friendly city guide to Ann Arbor, Michigan. Browse local restaurants, shops, events, parks, and neighborhood stories in one place, and save your favorites to your own "My Vibe" page.

A2 Eats

Curated restaurants, bars, cafes, and eateries around Ann Arbor. Search by name, cuisine, or neighborhood, and see featured spots.

Local Shops & Dispensaries

A directory of independent Ann Arbor retail, including bookstores and vinyl record shops, vintage clothing and boutiques, specialty food markets, and licensed cannabis dispensaries (21+). Listings include addresses, hours, and links to directions.

A2 Events

Upcoming events and happenings, filterable by month and category: festivals, nightlife and live music, museums, parks, workshops, sports, family-friendly activities, tours, arts and culture, and hidden gems.

A2 Guide: Stories & Local Tips

Articles and field notes on city life, local secrets, arts and culture, dining reviews, community reports, and meetups. Residents can also submit stories and tips to a community board and comment on each other's posts.

Ann Arbor Park Finder

Find parks and nature areas such as Nichols Arboretum, Gallup Park, Bird Hills Nature Area, Matthaei Botanical Gardens, Barton Nature Area, and Bandemer Park. Filter by dog-friendly, wheelchair-accessible trails, playgrounds, bike paths, and restrooms.

Getting Around Ann Arbor

Quick links to local transportation, including TheRide (AAATA) buses and trip planner, FlexRide on-demand rides, and local taxi service.

Tools for locals and visitors

  • "What To Do Today" itineraries for a chill Saturday, date night, or campus exploring
  • Hot spots and landmarks like Michigan Stadium, the Law Quad, and Kerrytown
  • A random restaurant picker, Ann Arbor trivia, and a bucket list
  • A tip calculator, monthly weather averages, and a hydration tracker

Local businesses and event organizers can apply to be listed or featured on A2 Vibe. Sign in with Google to save favorites and join the community board.
